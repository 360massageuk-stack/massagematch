RELAXATION MASSAGE FAQ

Useful things to know

What is relaxation massage?

Relaxation massage usually focuses on a calmer, less intense treatment experience intended to help you unwind.

How is relaxation massage different from deep tissue massage?

Relaxation massage is generally gentler, while deep tissue massage tends to involve more focused work on muscular tension.

Can I compare relaxation massage prices?

Yes. Therapist profiles can include treatment durations and prices so you can compare options.

How do I arrange an appointment?

Contact the therapist directly through the details shown on their MassageMatch profile.
