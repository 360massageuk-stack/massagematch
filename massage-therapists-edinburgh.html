EDINBURGH MASSAGE FAQ

Useful things to know

How do I find a massage therapist in Edinburgh?

Search Edinburgh by treatment or service, compare therapist profiles, prices and working locations, then contact the therapist directly.

Do I book and pay through MassageMatch?

No. MassageMatch is a directory. You contact the therapist directly to ask about availability and arrange your appointment.

Can I compare massage prices?

Therapist profiles can show treatments, durations and prices, helping you compare options before making contact.

Does MassageMatch charge a booking fee?

No. Customers are not charged a MassageMatch booking fee, and therapists are contacted directly.
