WHY MASSAGEMATCH?

Created by a therapist.
Built for therapists.

MassageMatch was created in Scotland by a massage therapist who understood first-hand how frustrating advertising a legitimate massage business could be.

General classified platforms weren't designed around our profession. Advertising can be expensive, rules can be confusing, and legitimate listings can sometimes disappear without it being clear what needs to change.

MassageMatch is new, and every great directory has to start somewhere. We are not pretending there are thousands of professionals here already. We are building this properly, one independent therapist and one genuine profile at a time.

By joining early, you are not just supporting a new UK platform. You are helping shape a directory designed to help independent professionals get discovered without unnecessary commission or inflated advertising costs.

Our first members will be recognised as Founding Therapists. Thank you for taking a chance on something new and helping us build something that can, in turn, help your business grow.

Fair pricing. Clear rules. A human approach.

— Founder, MassageMatch UK
