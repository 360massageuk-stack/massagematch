WHY MASSAGEMATCH?

Created by a therapist.
Built for therapists.

MassageMatch was created in Scotland by a massage therapist who understood first-hand how frustrating advertising a legitimate massage business could be.

General classified platforms weren't designed around our profession. Advertising can be expensive, rules can be confusing, and legitimate listings can sometimes disappear without it being clear what needs to change.

So we're building something different: a dedicated UK home where professional therapists can create a great profile, explain what they offer and connect directly with people genuinely looking for massage.

Fair pricing. Clear rules. A human approach.

— Founder, MassageMatch UK
