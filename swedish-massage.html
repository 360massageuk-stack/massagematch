SWEDISH MASSAGE FAQ

Useful things to know

What is Swedish massage?

Swedish massage is a classic style of massage commonly associated with relaxation, general wellbeing and flowing massage techniques.

Is Swedish massage suitable if I want a relaxing treatment?

It is often chosen for relaxation, although the exact pressure and approach vary by therapist.

Can I compare Swedish massage prices?

Yes. MassageMatch profiles can show treatment durations and prices so you can compare options before making contact.

How do I arrange an appointment?

MassageMatch is a directory, so you contact the therapist directly to ask about availability and arrange your appointment.
