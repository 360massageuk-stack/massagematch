SPORTS MASSAGE FAQ

Useful things to know

What is sports massage used for?

People often choose sports massage for focused muscular work, training recovery, movement and general physical maintenance.

Do I need to be an athlete to book sports massage?

No. Individual therapists may work with a wide range of clients, not only competitive athletes.

Can I compare sports massage prices?

Yes. Therapist profiles can show treatment durations and prices so you can compare options before making contact.

Do I book through MassageMatch?

No. MassageMatch is a directory. You contact the therapist directly.
